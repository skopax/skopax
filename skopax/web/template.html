    How this works

    SourcesEvery 30 minutes, read only: Prometheus and the other sources that are configured. Each source on the overview card opens its page.
    RulesEach hour is compared with the same hour 1 d and 7 d earlier: a count is flagged above twice that level plus a minimum gap, latency also needs +250 ms, and a service stays quiet 30 minutes after a deploy. Alerts, restarts, volumes and other pod checks have their own floors. Only new or worse critical and warning items notify. Times are in your browser's time zone.
    ActionsInvestigate, Mute, Known noise and Post-deploy check queue an action. Investigate goes to the investigator, which starts it within about two minutes (in shadow mode it only proposes); a queued one can be cancelled until it starts. Mute, Known noise and Resolve apply at once. Post-deploy check waits for the next manual check. Resolve closes an anomaly as Fixed, No action needed or a Duplicate of another card (its number or key), and the card history shows how, via the page, chat, Slack or [[brand]] itself, and who; a stopped anomaly stays under Open until it is resolved, marked known noise or muted. Send to Slack posts the anomaly to the channel right away, from the service's own Slack app; when the card has a Slack draft, the draft is the message and the anomaly card goes under it. Deploy reports have the same Send to Slack for the whole report. Copy link gives a link that opens that anomaly, release or deploy report. Ask the investigator (operators, in the Investigator menu at the top or on Requests) opens a private chat with the question; the answer lands in that chat. Ask about this card, under Runs on this card in an expanded card, asks about that card alone and shows the answer there.
    Links and SlackPages, anomalies, releases and reports each have a link (#t=, #a=, #rel=, #r=). A coloured row in the sidebar has something to look at; hover it for why. Send to Slack posts from [[brand]]'s own Slack app; drafts on cards and deploy reports go out within 30 s unless held (Notifications). The Activity rail shows what the server and the investigator are doing, as it happens.
