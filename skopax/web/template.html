      Get started
      • A card is one problem in production. Alerts and errors about the same thing are grouped on one card, so the team can see who is on it.
      • The Board lists the cards. The filters under Board in the sidebar pick which ones you see.
      • Openfiring, or stopped with nobody acting on it yet. The Board starts here.
      • Stopped firingthe problem went quiet, but the card stays open until it is resolved.
      • In progresssomeone started work on the card.
      • Mutedcards someone muted.
      • Critical, HighandWarningopen cards of that severity.
      • Severity goes Critical, High, Warning, then Info. High means users feel it now, such as errors, a broken page or a failed deploy. A card whose numbers double moves one level up.
      • Recurringcards that keep coming back with no Jira ticket on them.
      • Archivedcards that are not firing, or muted, with no change for a while; they are hidden everywhere else.
      • Closedcards that already left the page, kept for a year.
      • With a new card, read it first: what fired, its numbers and the investigator's note. Then choose Start progress, Assign, Investigate or Resolve.

      A card's life
      • A card fires while the problem is there. When it goes quiet it reads Stopped firing and stays under Open until someone resolves it, or it closes by itself.
      • A card resolved as Fixed that fires again came back: it is posted at once as at least High and goes back to the person who resolved it. A card resolved as No action needed reopens on the board only, unless it got worse.
      • A stopped card that nobody acted on closes by itself after a set time per severity (Admin > Settings).
      • A card assigned to someone waits three times as long before it closes by itself, so its person has time to act.
      • MuteandKnown noisesilence a card for a while or for good; see Card actions.
      • Groupmerges a card under another open card, the one that explains it. The other card becomes its parent.
      • Reopenputs a card back to Open, like dragging its tile to the Open column.
      • A card posted to Slack that nobody has started, resolved or muted gets a reminder in its Slack thread after a set number of hours.
      • For an assigned card, the reminder names the person who has it.

      Card actions
      • Start progressthe card goes In progress with you as owner. With Jira connected you can add a ticket.
      • Assignpick the person who owns the card; its status does not change. With Slack and the Assignee message switch on, they get a Slack message with the link.
      • Investigatethe investigator takes the card within about two minutes. If it ran before, you are asked first.
      • Resolvecloses the card with one of three choices:
      • Fixedthe cause is gone because something was changed, such as a deploy, a config change or a rollback.
      • No action neededharmless or a one-off, nothing was changed.
      • Duplicatethe same problem as another card. Give its number, like 12, or its key.
      • Mute 24 handMute 1 wmute the card for 24 hours or 7 days. Unmute undoes it.
      • Known noisemutes a card for good, with a reason. Use it for something you expect.
      • Post-deploy checkon Deploy reports, starts a check of the recent deploys and writes a deploy report when there are any.
      • Send to Slackposts the card to the Slack channel right away. After that the button reads Send again.
      • Copy linkcopies a link that opens this card on the Board.
      • Ask about this cardthe question box on a card. It starts a private chat about the card on the Chat page.
      • ExpandandShrinkmake the side panel larger or smaller.

      The investigator
      • The investigator reads your metrics, logs and recent deploys through the connected integrations. It writes why a card fired and what to do next.
      • Its mode is off, shadow or live. In shadow mode it only records what it would write. In live mode it writes.
      • Admin > Investigator sets the mode.
      • Ask the investigatoroperators find it in the Investigator menu at the top or on Requests. It opens a private chat with the question, and the answer lands in that chat.
      • The Chat page answers questions about prod from the connected integrations and names what it read. It needs Chat on in Admin > Features.
      • With Slack connected and the Slack listener on (Admin > Features), you can ask in Slack too.
      • A daily dollar limit, shared with the self-review, stops the investigator until the next day once it is reached (Admin > Investigator).
      • The Investigator page shows what runs now and what waits. History, under Activity in the sidebar, lists the investigator runs, and Costs shows what they cost.
      • The Activity rail shows what the server and the investigator are doing, as it happens.

      Notifications and messages
      • The bell in the top bar opens Notifications: what happened on the cards you hold and on your chat answers. Opening the page marks them read.
      • Preferences > Email: Email me my notifications is on for every kind once an admin sets up a Mail server. Switch it off, or untick the kinds you do not want. Mail goes only to the address of your own account.
      • While Slack auto-send is on, new and worse cards from a set severity up are posted to the Slack channel on their own.
      • When the investigator finds a card or a deploy report worth telling, it posts to Slack itself.
      • A Slack post that did not go out is listed under Not posted to Slack on History, under Activity in the sidebar, with Send now and View.
      • A Microsoft Teams route or an Email route sends chosen cards, by tag or key, to a Teams channel or a mail list. Send one by hand from each card's Notify row.

      Trace [[brand]] with Langfuse
      • [[brand]] uses Langfuse in two ways. A Langfuse instance can watch the project of your AI app and raise lf-* cards, or receive the traces of [[brand]]'s own AI calls. The instance that receives the traces is not watched.
      • In Langfuse, the API keys decide the project: [[brand]] sends to the project its keys belong to, and has no project field.
      • Create a projectin Langfuse, create a separate Langfuse project for [[brand]]'s traces, such as skopax. Do not use the project of an app [[brand]] watches: the instance you pick stops being watched, and the traces would mix into the app's numbers.
      • Create keysin that project, open Settings > API Keys and create a key pair. Keep Full access: a Viewer (read-only) key cannot send traces.
      • Add the instanceon Connect > Integrations, add a Langfuse instance with a name, the URL of Langfuse Cloud or your own server, and the two keys. Leave Environment, Namespace, Service and Turn name as they are; they are only for watching. Test connection names the project of the keys. Then Save & turn on.
      • Pick itin Admin > Investigator > Langfuse tracing, pick the instance. Trace content metadata sends no text; full also sends prompts and answers, which can hold customer data. Traces sampled at 100 sends every trace.
      • Switch it onin Admin > Features, switch on Langfuse tracing. While something is missing, the switch says what.
      • Check itthe instance now says it receives Skopax's own traces, so it is not watched. Run Investigate on a card, find its trace under Traces in the Langfuse project, and open it from Open trace on the Investigator page.Read more
      • Nothing shows upcheck that the keys come from the right project and may write, that Traces sampled is 100, and that the run made a model call. Traces go out within seconds after a run ends.Read more

      Words
      • Cardone problem in production, with its alerts, errors and history.
      • Instanceone connection of an integration, such as one Prometheus per cluster.
      • Integrationa plug-in connected on Connect > Integrations, such as Prometheus or Slack.
      • Routean integration that sends chosen cards to a Teams channel or a mail list.
      • Workspacea whole other deployment, such as a staging copy. It is not an instance.
      • Known noisea card muted for good, with a reason, because it is expected.
      • Shadow modethe investigator only records what it would write.
      • Responsiblea person who looks after the cards with a given tag. The daily report names them.
